小鲸鱼 is an AI teammate for single-player Terraria. Follow, attack, pick up items, and equip gear using local commands. Connect an LLM to give natural-language instructions and plan multi-step tasks.

Click your teammate's name in the lower-right corner, press O, or enter /ai panel for inventory, settings, and quick commands. Set a name under Teammate name and use it to address your teammate in Terraria's chat box.

小鲸鱼是单人泰拉瑞亚的 AI 队友。在游戏聊天框输入「小鲸鱼，」后直接说话。点击右下角的队友名字、按 O 或输入 /ai panel 打开面板。可以在设置里改名、配置模型，也可以直接使用跟随、攻击和拾取等离线指令。

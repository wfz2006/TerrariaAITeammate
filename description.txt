Xiaoxing is an AI teammate for single-player Terraria. Follow, attack, pick up items, and equip gear using local commands. Connect an LLM to give natural-language instructions and plan multi-step tasks.

Click Xiaoxing in the lower-right corner, press O, or enter /ai panel for inventory, settings, and quick commands. Talk to your teammate by starting a message with @Xiaoxing in Terraria's chat box.
